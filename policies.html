Before you order

Store information

We have not published fixed delivery, returns, privacy or payment terms here because those business terms need to be confirmed by the shop. Please contact us for the current terms before purchase.

Prices, stock and product fitment

Some catalogue prices are examples supplied for demonstration and are not confirmed selling prices. Other items are priced on request. Product availability and vehicle compatibility must be confirmed with the shop. Adding an item to the cart does not reserve stock or create an accepted order.

Collection and delivery

Our listed location is Social Street, opposite Winstar Hotel, Eldoret. Open directions in Maps. Ask the shop whether collection is available, whether delivery can be arranged to your location, and confirm any fee and timing before ordering.

Payment

This website does not take payment or connect to M-Pesa or a payment provider. Discuss currently supported payment methods directly with the shop. Do not treat a cart or browser message as proof that payment or an order has been completed.

Returns, refunds and cancellations

Ask the shop for the applicable terms before purchase, especially for vehicle-specific parts, installed items, incorrect fitment, damaged products and refunds. Request confirmation of any agreed terms in writing.

Privacy

The cart is stored in your browser on this device. Checkout details are used to compose a WhatsApp inquiry and are not submitted to or stored by this static website. WhatsApp is a third-party service with its own privacy terms. Avoid including sensitive personal or payment information in messages.
