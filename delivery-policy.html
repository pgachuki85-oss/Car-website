Delivery Policy

We deliver within Eldoret and can arrange deliveries to other Kenyan towns depending on product availability and courier arrangements.

Local delivery charges are determined based on the order value and destination, and we explain the exact fee during checkout before placing an order.

Orders are typically prepared within 24 to 48 hours, subject to stock availability. Customers can also collect their purchases directly from our Eldoret shop.

We will confirm the expected delivery timeline once the order has been confirmed and payment arrangement is finalised.
