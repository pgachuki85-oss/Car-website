Returns & Refunds

Returns are considered only for products that are damaged, incorrect, or not compatible with the customer’s stated vehicle requirement.

Customers should contact us as soon as possible to report a problem, and we will advise whether the item can be exchanged, replaced, or refunded.

Items must be returned in their original condition and packaging where possible. Some products may be excluded if they are custom ordered or non-returnable by nature.

Refunds are processed after verification and may take a few business days depending on the payment method used.
